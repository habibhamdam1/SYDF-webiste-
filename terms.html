1. Acceptance of Terms

By registering as a member or using this website, you agree to be bound by these Terms and Conditions. If you do not agree, please do not use our services.

2. Membership Eligibility

  1. Members must be at least 15 years of age.
  2. Must be a resident of Malakand Division (for current members) or a designated region as we expand.
  3. Must provide accurate personal information.
  4. Must complete the interview process when required.
  5. Must be approved by the admin team.

3. Membership Fee

  1. The current monthly fee is Rs. 300 (subject to change with notice).
  2. Payment must be made via approved methods (JazzCash, EasyPaisa, Bank Transfer).
  3. Fee is due by the 5th of each month.
  4. Late payments may result in temporary account suspension.
  5. Fees are non-refundable.

4. Member Responsibilities

  1. Respect: Treat all members with respect regardless of gender, caste, or background.
  2. Accuracy: Keep your information accurate and up to date.
  3. Confidentiality: Do not share internal documents or discussions publicly.
  4. Privacy: Respect other members' privacy — do not share their contact info.
  5. Participation: Actively participate in activities when possible.
  6. Legal Compliance: Follow all Pakistani laws and SYDF bylaws.

5. Prohibited Conduct

The following actions will result in disciplinary action:

  1. Harassment, bullying, or discrimination of any kind
  2. Sharing inappropriate content
  3. Misusing member data or forum resources
  4. Misrepresenting SYDF in public
  5. Fraud, embezzlement, or misuse of funds
  6. Spreading false information about SYDF or its members
  7. Political or religious propaganda on our platform

6. Disciplinary Process

  1. Complaints are reviewed by the Discipline Committee.
  2. Member will be given a chance to explain.
  3. Actions may include: warning, suspension, or termination.
  4. Serious violations may involve law enforcement.

7. Cabinet & Leadership

  1. Cabinet positions are elected through democratic voting.
  2. Terms are generally 3 months (Nigran) or as decided.
  3. Cabinet members must fulfill role responsibilities.
  4. Positions can be revoked for misconduct.

8. Content Ownership

  1. All SYDF logos, documents, and content belong to the organization.
  2. Members may not use SYDF branding for personal purposes.
  3. Content shared by members (photos, stories) may be used by SYDF for promotional purposes.

9. Website Usage

  1. Do not attempt to hack or disrupt the website.
  2. Do not create multiple fake accounts.
  3. Do not use automated bots or scrapers.
  4. Login credentials must be kept secure.

10. Privacy

Your privacy is protected as per our Privacy Policy. Female members enjoy additional privacy protections.

11. Termination of Membership

  1. Members may voluntarily leave by contacting admin.
  2. SYDF may terminate membership for violations.
  3. Upon termination, all SYDF benefits cease immediately.
  4. Outstanding dues must still be cleared.
