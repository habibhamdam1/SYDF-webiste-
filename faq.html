How Can We Help?

Find answers to the most common questions about SYDF.

👥 Membership

How can I become a member of SYDF?

+

Simply visit our Membership page and fill out the application form. Once membership is open, you'll be contacted for a short interview. After approval, you'll receive your unique Member ID (UID) and access to the member dashboard.

What is the membership fee?

+

The current monthly membership fee is Rs. 300. This can be paid via JazzCash, EasyPaisa, or Bank Transfer. The fee may change based on forum decisions.

Can female members join SYDF?

+

Absolutely! SYDF has a dedicated Women Wing. We ensure privacy with options like photo-optional profiles, restricted contact visibility, and dedicated support for female members.

Why is membership sometimes closed?

+

Membership opens periodically based on our capacity and current needs. When closed, you can leave your details on the waitlist, and we'll notify you via email when it opens again.

💰 Payments

What payment methods are accepted?

+

We accept JazzCash, EasyPaisa, and Bank Transfer. After payment, you should send the receipt or screenshot to admin for record updating.

How do I get my payment receipt?

+

All payment receipts are available in your dashboard under "My Payments". You can download any receipt as PDF anytime.

What happens if I miss a payment?

+

You'll receive a reminder notification. If payments remain overdue for an extended period, your account may be temporarily suspended until cleared.

🎯 Activities

How can I join SYDF activities?

+

All upcoming activities are listed on the Activities page. Members receive notifications via email, WhatsApp, and dashboard. Simply register or show up at the venue.

Can I propose a new activity?

+

Yes! We encourage members to propose activities. Contact your district coordinator or use the Feedback form to share your idea. The cabinet will review it.

📄 Documents & Privacy

Can I view other districts' documents?

+

No. For security and privacy, members only have access to documents related to their district and role. This ensures sensitive information stays protected.

Who can see my personal information?

+

Only the Super Admin has access to your full details (CNIC, phone, email). Other members can only see your name, photo (if you allow), and district.

Is my photo required for membership?

+

Photo is optional, especially for female members. If you choose not to upload a photo, a default avatar will be shown on your profile.

⚙️ General

What areas does SYDF cover?

+

We are currently active across 9 districts of Malakand Division: Swat, Shangla, Buner, Malakand, Upper Dir, Lower Dir, Upper Chitral, Lower Chitral, and Bajaur. We are expanding to other parts of Pakistan soon.

How can I contact SYDF?

+

You can reach us via the Contact page, email us at info@SYDF.org, or call/WhatsApp us. Contact details are available in the Contact section.

Can I donate or sponsor SYDF?

+

Yes! Visit our Donate page for donations (always open) or Sponsor page to sponsor specific activities. Every contribution supports youth empowerment.

Still have questions?

Our team is happy to help you.
