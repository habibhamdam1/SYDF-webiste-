🔒 Privacy Policy

Home / Privacy Policy

Introduction

SWAT Youth Development Forum (SYDF) is committed to protecting the privacy of our members and website visitors. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information.

Information We Collect

Personal Information

  • Full name, father's name
  • CNIC number
  • Date of birth
  • Email address and phone number
  • District and tehsil
  • Education and profession details
  • Profile photo (optional)

Automatic Information

  • Login timestamps
  • Device and browser information
  • IP address (for security)

How We Use Your Information

  • To process membership applications
  • To manage member records and payments
  • To send notifications and updates
  • To organize activities and events
  • To verify member identity when needed
  • To improve our services

Who Can See Your Information

Super Admin: Has access to all member information including CNIC, contact details, and photos.

Admins (President, VP, GS): Can see basic information and manage members, but cannot access sensitive data like CNIC.

Other Members: Can only see your name, UID, district, and photo (if you allow). Your email, phone, and CNIC are NEVER visible to other members.

Public Visitors: Cannot see any personal information.

Female Member Privacy

We provide enhanced privacy for female members:

  • Profile photo is optional
  • Photos (if uploaded) are only visible to Super Admin
  • Email and phone are only visible to Super Admin
  • Can choose to hide from public members directory

Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS)
  • Secure database storage
  • Role-based access control
  • Regular backups
  • Activity logging

Your Rights

  • Access your personal data anytime
  • Update or correct your information
  • Control your privacy settings
  • Request account deletion
  • Opt out of non-essential communications

Cookies

We use minimal cookies for essential functions like login sessions and preferences. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some features may not work properly.

Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services for:

  • Email delivery (Resend, EmailJS)
  • WhatsApp notifications
  • Payment processing (JazzCash, EasyPaisa, Banks)
  • Analytics (Google Analytics)

These services have their own privacy policies. We only share the minimum required information.

Children's Privacy

Our services are for individuals aged 15 and above. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 15.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Significant changes will be communicated to all members via email and website notifications.
